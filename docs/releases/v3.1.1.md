**ONYX v3.1.1: a new look. The game you pick fills the background, optional box art, critics' scores, six new ambient soundtracks and a smoother way through your library.**

What's new
- **The game fills the background.** Highlight a game and a faded picture of it (a gameplay screenshot, downloaded once) fades in behind the menu and drifts slowly. The old banner and screenshot slideshow are gone. Turn it off under Settings > Interface > "Game picture as the background".
- **Box art (optional).** Settings > Interface > "Box art" shows each game's box art instead of its banner picture, in the crossbar rows, the channel grid and a large cover beside the game's details. Box art is downloaded once per game from the libretro thumbnail project, for most US and European 3DS releases (and GBA and NES). Box art belongs to Nintendo.
- **More about each game.** The details panel now shows the critics' average as stars with the score, genre tags, the description, and the release, developer and publisher.
- **Smoother selection.** The list glides to the game you land on, the highlighted row slides toward the details, the details slide in, and opening a game fades the menu out over its picture.
- **Six new soundtracks.** Every theme has its own calm ambient track, written for ONYX; none share music any more. (Your own music in the Music folder still works.)
- **Fast vertex cache switch (for testing).** Settings > "Fast vertex cache" turns the 3D model cache off. If Mario Kart 7 characters or sky flicker black, or Smash models show noise, play a race or match with it off and with it on and tell me if anything changes: that is the one test that tells me whether the cache is to blame. Off is slower.
- **Credits** now list the thumbnail project and Wikipedia, and say that box art is Nintendo's.

Things to know
- Still open, honestly: the black OpenGL bottom screen, the Smash model/bottom-screen noise and the Mario Kart 7 sky/character flicker have no confirmed fix yet. This update adds the vertex cache switch above and a stale-data checksum guard (from 3.1.0), and the logs now say how the OpenGL screens are fed. Please send logs from those games.
- The background picture, scores and box art need an internet connection the first time. Everything is cached afterwards, and games the sources don't know are remembered so ONYX doesn't keep asking.
- Scores come from Wikipedia's review boxes, so some games have none.
- Not every game has a screenshot or box art online; those fall back to the banner or the card.
- Apart from the switch above, this update changes the menu only. Emulation speed is unchanged.

ALPHA software. Please send logs if anything breaks.
